Disc title index
================

assets/disc-index.tsv is a derived index containing titles and serials from the Redump
metadata DAT files in libretro/libretro-database:

- metadat/redump/Sony - PlayStation.dat
- metadat/redump/Sega - Saturn.dat

Source: https://github.com/libretro/libretro-database
The upstream repository identifies these datasets as Redump > TOSEC sources.
The database is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .
The TSV is a transformed selection of title and serial fields, and is provided under the
same license. It contains no game images or disc contents.

To refresh it, download those two DAT files and run:

  tools/generate-disc-index.py 'Sony - PlayStation.dat' 'Sega - Saturn.dat' assets/disc-index.tsv
